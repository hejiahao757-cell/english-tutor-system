七选五 · 开做前 60 秒方法提醒

① 先预测空位功能

它是总领、解释、转折、步骤、例证、让步还是总结？先判断“缺什么工作”，再看句子。

② 双向取证

空前找指代与逻辑入口，空后找复现与结果。正确答案必须前后都接得住。

③ 五锁

结构锁、指代锁、复现锁、逻辑锁、功能锁。通常两三把同时闭合才选。

④ 全文复核

五个答案放回后，检查段落推进和两个干扰项为什么始终没有合法位置。

七选五 1

Why Explaining Changes What You Know

为什么“讲出来”会改变你真正掌握的东西｜主题：学习与记忆

5空｜建议 8—10分钟
初高中衔接 · 说明文｜每篇独立批改
先看空前空后，再用“五锁一验”定位；正文与选项所有英文词都可点词卡。

Reading the same notes again can feel smooth because the words are already familiar. However, that comfortable feeling does not always reveal what you can explain by yourself. 1 When the page is closed, the brain must organise the idea instead of simply recognising it.

The method is simple. Choose one important idea, close your notes, and explain it as if you were speaking to a younger student. 2 Return only to that exact point, review it, and then try the explanation again.

Some learners dislike this method because they hesitate and feel less confident. 3 A pause often marks the place where a relation is weak or a detail has not been connected to the main idea. In this way, difficulty becomes useful information.

Explaining also forces you to show how ideas are related. You may need to say what caused a result, why two examples are different, or how one step leads to another. 4 That is why a short explanation can test comprehension more deeply than another quick review.

At the end of each week, repeat the process with the ideas that mattered most. 5 Your next review then has a clear direction instead of becoming another complete rereading. The goal is not to sound perfect; it is to discover what your own brain can rebuild.

A—G 选项

A
The important question is not whether the sentence looks familiar, but whether you can rebuild its meaning.
真正重要的问题不是这个句子看起来是否熟悉，而是你能不能重新搭建出它的意思。
B
In fact, the hesitation is not a failure; it is a signal.
事实上，犹豫并不是失败，而是一个信号。
C
Keep a short record of the places where your explanation breaks down.
把自己解释中断、讲不下去的地方简短记录下来。
D
Explaining is different because it removes the support of the page.
解释则不同，因为它会拿走页面提供的支撑。
E
Many students therefore prefer to copy every example in a different colour.
因此，许多学生更喜欢用不同颜色把每个例子都抄下来。
F
A useful explanation therefore contains relationships, not only separate definitions.
所以，一个真正有用的解释包含的是各种关系，而不只是一个个彼此分开的定义。
G
If you stop at one point, do not open the whole page at once.
如果你在某一点卡住了，不要立刻把整页笔记都重新打开。

七选五 2

The Ten-Minute Delay

把消息晚十分钟再看｜主题：科技与自我管理

5空｜建议 8—10分钟
初高中衔接 · 议论文/说明文｜每篇独立批改
先看空前空后，再用“五锁一验”定位；正文与选项所有英文词都可点词卡。

A message appears on a digital device while you are doing homework. Checking it may take only a few seconds, so the interruption seems harmless. 1 After the message is gone, part of your attention may still remain with the conversation instead of the task.

A simple experiment is to delay a non-urgent message for ten minutes. Finish the small task already in front of you: one paragraph, five questions, or one short review. 2 The message is still there, but your work now has a clear stopping point.

Of course, not every message should wait. A family emergency or an important change in a plan may require an immediate reply. 3 Once you make that distinction, fewer messages feel like commands.

The environment can also make the habit easier. Put the device a little farther away or turn it over so that every new message is not immediately visible. 4 You do not have to win the same battle with yourself every two minutes.

At first, waiting may feel uncomfortable because the old habit is still strong. 5 The purpose is not to become a person who never checks messages. It is to become the person who decides when attention moves.
