应用文 · City Activity Day

3题｜快速定位与信息匹配
CITY ACTIVITY DAY — Saturday, July 25

Place: East Lake Youth Centre

The centre will advertise four activities for students aged 13–16. Read each advertisement and choose what interests you.

A. PAST COMES ALIVE — 9:00, Main Hall

Meet an actor and an actress who will act out a lively scene from an ancient city. The cast will show the style of clothes worn by different groups. Each caption explains a main symbol, and the clothes represent different groups from the past. A student will give a five-minute presentation before the live show. After it, the audience may ask questions aloud through a loudspeaker. A school representative will record the activity for a later broadcast. Anyone who wants to admire old art or improve presentation ability may enjoy it. Each spectator may receive a small gift, and the centre will publish some questions later.

B. NATURE LAB — 10:20, Room 204

Take part in an inspection of leaves, weeds and small bushes. Inspect how a leaf surface can absorb water, use a brush to wipe away dust, and spray water to compare plants. The teacher will highlight how living things survive in the wild and how people can maintain a healthy natural environment. The room contains basic lab facilities and a machine that can enlarge pictures. Follow the standard procedure. At the end, students gather near the blackboard to compare results and learn the concept of sustainability.

C. CASTLE ADVENTURE — 14:00, City Museum

Get virtual access to an ancient castle and palace. The gallery is accessible to all students, and the centre can accept 30 visitors at a time. See vivid pictures, a memorial and a monument, and learn how each symbol can remind people of an ancestor. Then work together to escape from a “locked room”. Inspect a map, calculate the exact way, count the steps, ascend a short steps, pass a chain, descend, press the correct button and switch on a light. After a ten-second countdown, the final door opens. Behave well and follow all advice.

D. SURVIVAL ON WATER — 15:20, Training Pool

Learn how to act when trouble happens aboard a small boat. Before you board, a captain will check every life belt, rope and strap. Learn to fasten a loose strap, keep a rope tight, judge water depth, and react if someone falls below the surface. The captain will explain how body weight can affect balance. The main aim is to help students remain calm and take correct action instead of trying to fight the water. Students must be able to swim 25 metres.

• Write your name at the counter ten minutes early. The receptionist will give you a receipt. A calendar shows the usual activity times.

故事类 · The Rope by the River

4题｜情节、原因、人物选择与主旨
THE ROPE BY THE RIVER

Sixteen-year-old Ben joined a school adventure in a wide forest area near a river. The group contained eight students, two teachers and a local guide. Before they left, the guide made an inspection of their bags, checked that everyone had adequate water, and advised them to stay together, follow the marked way and never abandon the group for a better picture.

At first, everything was normal. The landscape was beautiful and the students were lively. Ben admired the deep green jungle and wild birds above the trees. He took pictures of every bush, weed and strange plant he encountered. He was so absorbed in the scenery that he did not perceive the others moving ahead.

When Ben looked up, the group was absent. He called aloud, but there was no reaction. His heart seemed to sink. He began to doubt whether he had followed the correct way. The situation was terrible and frightening, but he recalled the guide’s advice: “Do not turn one problem into two.”

Ben remained where he was and assessed what he had. His phone could not work, but his bag contained water, a bright cloth and a long string. He tied the cloth to a branch and called aloud three times.

Soon he heard a cry from the other side of the river. A fellow student, Sam, had slipped and hurt his leg. Two students were with him, but the water was becoming deeper. Ben saw a rescue rope fixed to a strong tree, part of an old rescue facility.

Ben did not cross. He told the others to remain calm. He inspected the rope, found one end loose and tightened it around the tree. Then he threw the free end across a small part of the river. Sam’s friends fastened it around another tree. The rope was not a bridge, but its strength gave them something strong to hold while they moved to safer ground. Ben warned them not to rush because one wrong action could increase the threat.

The teachers soon arrived after hearing Ben’s calls. Sam’s leg had begun to swell, but there was no serious damage or other symptom. Everyone returned safely. Ben later said that the scene had looked horrible, but calm action had reduced the danger.

The guide praised Ben. ““Being brave does not mean you are not afraid,” he said. “It means you still have the ability to act well.””

Ben’s main achievement was not completing a spectacular venture. It was learning that correct action and respect for rules can strengthen a group and help people survive trouble together.

河边的绳子

16岁的本参加了一次学校组织的冒险活动，地点在一条河附近的大片森林区域。队伍里有8名学生、2名老师和1名当地向导。出发前，向导检查了大家的背包，确认每个人都有足够的水，并建议大家始终待在一起，沿着标记好的路线前进，不要只是为了拍到更好的照片就擅自离开队伍。

一开始一切都很正常。风景很美，学生们也很活跃。本很欣赏深绿色的丛林和树上方飞过的野鸟。他给每一片灌木、杂草和遇到的奇怪植物拍照。他太专注于风景，以至于没有察觉其他人已经往前走了。

当本抬起头时，队伍已经不见了。他大声呼喊，却没有任何回应。他的心仿佛一下沉了下去。他开始怀疑自己是否走对了路。情况很糟糕，也让人害怕，但他想起了向导的建议：“不要把一个问题变成两个问题。”

本留在原地，先判断自己有什么。他的手机无法使用，但包里有水、一块亮色布和一根长绳。他把布系在树上，然后大声呼喊了三次。

很快，他听见河对岸传来声音。同组学生山姆滑倒并伤到了腿。还有两名学生和他在一起，但河水越来越深。本看见附近一棵结实的树上固定着一根救援绳，那是河边旧救援设施的一部分。

本没有贸然过河，而是让大家保持冷静。他检查绳子，发现一端松了，就把它在树上系得更紧，然后把另一端扔过河面较窄的一段。山姆的同伴把绳子固定在另一棵树上。绳子并不能变成真正的桥，但它足够结实，让大家在慢慢向更安全的地方移动时有东西可以抓住。本提醒他们不要着急，因为一个错误行动就可能增加危险。

老师们听到本的呼喊后很快赶到。山姆的腿开始肿起来，但没有严重损伤，也没有其他症状。所有人都安全返回。本后来表示，那几分钟的场面看起来非常可怕，但冷静行动降低了危险。

向导表扬了本。“勇敢并不意味着你不会害怕，”他说，“而是意味着即使害怕，你仍然有能力采取正确行动。”

本当天最重要的收获并不是完成了一次刺激的冒险，而是明白了：正确行动和尊重规则能够让团队更有力量，并帮助大家一起从困境中安全走出来。

说明议论类 · Why Recalling Helps Memory

4题｜原因、指代、方法与主旨
WHY RECALLING HELPS MEMORY

Many students use a common review method: reading a list again and again until every word looks familiar. This approach feels easy, which is its main advantage. However, familiar is not equal to remembered. The actual effect of learning can differ from the feeling of learning—an important aspect students often fail to notice.

Imagine two students with equal ability studying the same 40 words for the same length of time. Student A reads the list aloud five times. Student B reads it once, closes the book, and tries to recall each meaning. When Student B cannot remember, he checks the word and tries again. Both make an equal effort altogether, but their actions differ.

A sceptical student may doubt that such a small change matters and expect a faster result. Yet the second approach can be more efficient because memory becomes stronger when the brain must search for information. Each try to recall a word is a small exercise. This active process can strengthen memory of a word and its meaning. Effort can accumulate into real progress. A learner may not achieve perfect recall at once, but repeated success can help accomplish the larger goal.

Active recall also shows what you actually know. Reading alone may create a wrong expectation of knowledge because words look familiar. Closing the book reveals the absence of an answer. That small problem is useful information: it highlights the exact words that need attention and enables students to assess their knowledge more clearly.

Good review also needs time. Knowledge does not become perfect in one day. A word may be remembered today, partly forgotten tomorrow, then recalled again later. This is normal. Each successful recall helps memory remain longer. The procedure may look slow, but regular practice can facilitate steady advance.

No single method is perfect for every person or every type of word. Some students benefit from pictures; others use examples, exchange ideas with a fellow student, or use words in a presentation. A certain method may have an advantage for one learner and a disadvantage for another. The priority is to assess its effect. Students can even speculate which words are likely to cause trouble and review those first.

Teachers also influence this process. An influential teacher does not simply give answers. Instead of punishing every wrong answer, the teacher may advise students to explain why an answer is wrong or use a word in a new scene. Such activities add depth to learning and make knowledge more accessible.

The goal is not to become a “perfect machine” that never forgets. It is to reduce the time needed to understand a familiar word when you encounter it again. As this process becomes quicker, reading becomes smoother and students can focus on the main idea and how the ideas fit together. Small regular efforts may not look spectacular, but they can have a broad impact on a confident attitude, ambition and the prospect of success. Students who become capable of quick recall can certainly be more confident when they read and are more likely to succeed.

为什么主动回忆有助于记忆

很多学生复习英语单词时会使用一种常见方法：一遍又一遍地读单词表，直到每个词看起来都很熟悉。这种方法感觉很轻松，这是它的主要优势。然而，“看起来熟悉”并不等于“真正记住”。学习的实际效果可能和学习时的感觉不同——这是学生经常忽视的一个重要方面。

设想两名能力相当的学生，用相同时间学习同样的40个单词。学生A把单词表大声读五遍。学生B读一遍后合上书，然后尝试回忆每个词的意思。只要想不起来，他就检查一下，再尝试一次。两个人总投入的努力相同，但采取的行动不同。

一个持怀疑态度的学生可能会怀疑这么小的改变有没有用，并期待更快的结果。但第二种方法可能更高效，因为当大脑必须主动寻找信息时，记忆会变得更强。每一次尝试回忆一个单词，都像一次小练习。这种积极的过程能够加强对单词及其含义的记忆。努力会逐渐积累成真正的进步。学习者不一定马上达到完美回忆，但一次次成功可以帮助完成更大的目标。

主动回忆还能显示你真正掌握了什么。只阅读可能会产生错误的“我已经会了”的预期，因为这些词看起来很熟悉。合上书后，想不出答案的问题就会暴露出来。这个小问题其实是有用的信息：它会突出真正需要注意的单词，并让学生更清楚地评估自己的知识掌握情况。

好的复习还需要时间。知识通常不会在一天内变得完美。一个词今天可能记得，明天忘掉一部分，之后又再次想起来，这是正常的。每一次成功回忆都能帮助记忆保持更长时间。这个过程看起来可能比较慢，但规律练习能够促进稳定进步。

没有一种方法对所有人、所有类型的单词都是完美的。有些学生从图片中受益，有些人喜欢看例句、和同学交换想法，或者把新词用于简短展示。某一种方法对一个学习者可能有优势，对另一个人却可能有劣势。最优先考虑的是评估方法的效果，而不是它感觉起来有多舒服。学生甚至可以推测哪些词最容易带来麻烦，然后先复习这些词。

老师也会影响这个过程。有影响力的老师不会只是直接给答案。与其因为每个错误答案而处罚学生，老师更可以建议学生解释答案为什么错，或者把单词放进新的场景里使用。这些活动会增加学习深度，让知识在不同情况下更容易被调用。

目标并不是变成一台永远不会遗忘的“完美机器”，而是当你再次遇到熟悉单词时，缩短理解它所需要的时间。随着这个过程越来越快，阅读会越来越顺，学生也能把更多注意力放到文章大意以及不同观点如何联系起来。小而规律的努力看起来也许并不惊人，但长期下来，它们会对自信的学习态度、志向和成功前景产生广泛影响。能够快速回忆的学生，在阅读时往往会更加自信，也更容易取得进步。

句子语感训练

重点句英译中 · 10题

先不要逐词写中文。建议顺序：第一眼说大意 → 找主干 → 划意群 → 自然表达。讲解模式下可逐句展开翻译流程。

01

A school representative will record the activity for a later broadcast.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程
① 第一眼抓主干

A school representative will record the activity

② 按意群切开

A school representative / will record the activity / for a later broadcast

③ 简单语法点

will + 动词原形表示将来；for a later broadcast 表示“为了之后的播出”。

④ 自然中文

学校的一名代表会把这次活动录下来，供之后播出。

语感目标：第一眼只抓“谁—做什么—为了什么”：代表 → 录活动 → 以后播出。
02

The main aim is to help students remain calm and take correct action instead of trying to fight the water.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程
① 第一眼抓主干

The main aim is to help students...

② 按意群切开

The main aim / is to help students remain calm / and take correct action / instead of trying to fight the water

③ 简单语法点

help sb do sth；instead of doing 表示“而不是做……”。

④ 自然中文

主要目标是帮助学生保持冷静并采取正确行动，而不是试图和水硬抗。

语感目标：看到 instead of，立刻感受“应该做什么 vs 不应该做什么”的对比。
03

He was so absorbed in the scenery that he did not perceive the others moving ahead.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程
① 第一眼抓主干

He was so absorbed ... that he did not perceive...

② 按意群切开

He was so absorbed in the scenery / that / he did not perceive / the others moving ahead

③ 简单语法点

so...that... 表示“如此……以至于……”；perceive sb doing 表示察觉某人正在做某事。

④ 自然中文

他太专注于眼前的风景，以至于没有察觉其他人已经向前走了。

语感目标：直接抓因果：太专注看风景 → 没注意队伍走远。
04

The situation was terrible and frightening, but he recalled the guide’s advice.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程
① 第一眼抓主干

The situation was terrible and frightening, but he recalled...

② 按意群切开

The situation was terrible and frightening / but / he recalled the guide’s advice

③ 简单语法点

but 后面发生转折，阅读时真正推动故事发展的信息通常在 but 后。

④ 自然中文

当时的情况很糟糕，也让人害怕，但他想起了向导的建议。

语感目标：不是逐词翻译，而是抓“害怕 → 但是想起建议”的转折。
05

The rope was not a bridge, but its strength gave them something strong to hold while they moved to safer ground.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程
① 第一眼抓主干

The rope was not a bridge, but its strength gave them something...

② 按意群切开

The rope was not a bridge / but its strength gave them something strong to hold / while they moved to safer ground

③ 简单语法点

something + 形容词 + to do；while 表示“当……的时候”。

④ 自然中文

这根绳子并不是一座桥，但它足够结实，让他们在向更安全的地方移动时有东西可以抓住。

语感目标：先形成画面：绳子不能当桥，却能提供支撑。
06

Being brave does not mean you are not afraid. It means you still have the ability to act well.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程
① 第一眼抓主干

Being brave does not mean... It means...

② 按意群切开

Being brave / does not mean / you are not afraid // It means / you still have the ability / to act well

③ 简单语法点

动名词 Being brave 作主语；does not mean... / It means... 用来纠正一种错误理解。

④ 自然中文

勇敢并不意味着你不会害怕，而是意味着即使害怕，你仍然有能力采取正确行动。

语感目标：核心不是“无所畏惧”，而是“害怕时仍然做对的事”。
07

However, familiar is not equal to remembered.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程
① 第一眼抓主干

familiar is not equal to remembered

② 按意群切开

However / familiar / is not equal to / remembered

③ 简单语法点

however 表转折；be equal to 表示“等同于”。这里把 familiar 和 remembered 当成两种状态比较。

④ 自然中文

语感目标：一句话抓住全文核心：熟悉感 ≠ 真正记忆。

That small problem is useful information: it highlights the exact words that need attention and enables students to assess their knowledge more clearly.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程

② 按意群切开

That small problem / is useful information / it highlights the exact words that need attention / and enables students to assess their knowledge more clearly

③ 简单语法点

that need attention 是定语从句修饰 words；enable sb to do sth 表示“使某人能够做……”。

④ 自然中文

09

A certain method may have an advantage for one learner and a disadvantage for another.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程
① 第一眼抓主干

A certain method may have an advantage ... and a disadvantage...

② 按意群切开

A certain method / may have an advantage for one learner / and a disadvantage for another

③ 简单语法点

one...another... 表示“一个……另一个……”；advantage 与 disadvantage 构成对比。

④ 自然中文

某一种方法对一个学习者来说可能有优势，但对另一个学习者来说可能有劣势。

10

The goal is not to become a “perfect machine” that never forgets. It is to reduce the time needed to understand a familiar word when you encounter it again.

显示翻译流程收起翻译流程

② 按意群切开

The goal / is not to become a “perfect machine” that never forgets // It is to reduce the time / needed to understand a familiar word / when you encounter it again

③ 简单语法点

that never forgets 修饰 machine；needed to understand... 是过去分词短语修饰 time；when 引导时间状语。

④ 自然中文

目标并不是变成一台永远不会遗忘的“完美机器”，而是当你再次遇到一个熟悉单词时，缩短理解它所需要的时间。

语感目标：真正目标：不是永不忘，而是下次见到时反应更快。
